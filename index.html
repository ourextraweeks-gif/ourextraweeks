How we ended up with too much of a good thing

Over the years, our family and a close group of friends got a little carried away buying timeshare weeks at some genuinely spectacular resorts: Westin Ka'anapali on Maui, Westin Kierland in Scottsdale, and Garza Blanca in Puerto Vallarta, Cancún and Los Cabos. We love every one of them. The problem is, there are only so many weeks in a year.

Rather than let these weeks sit unused, we rent them out to fellow travellers who want a luxury resort experience without the ownership commitment. You get a stunning one or two bedroom villa at a top-tier resort; we get to feel slightly better about our timeshare habit. Everyone wins.

We handle the name-change paperwork and reservation transfer ourselves. No third-party broker fees, no markups. You're dealing directly with the owners, so communication is fast and personal.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rent a Westin Ka'anapali, Westin Kierland or Garza Blanca timeshare week directly from the owner?

Yes. Our Extra Weeks is a family and a small group of friends who own timeshare weeks at the Westin Ka'anapali Ocean Resort Villas and Villas North (Maui), the Westin Kierland Villas (Scottsdale), and Garza Blanca (Puerto Vallarta, Cancún and Los Cabos). We have been renting the weeks we can't use directly to travellers since 2016, with no broker fees, and we transfer the reservation into your name with the resort.

How much does a week cost?

It depends on the week, resort and dates. Prices are for the whole 7-night week in US dollars. Check the available weeks on this page for current dates and prices.

Are there taxes or fees on top of the price?

Your price covers the full week's rent. Each resort collects its own government taxes at check-in or check-out, paid directly to the resort:

  • Westin Ka'anapali (Maui), 2-bedroom villas: Hawaii's transient accommodations tax on timeshare stays, currently about US$35–40 per night (roughly US$250–270 for the week).
  • Westin Ka'anapali North, premium studio: the same Hawaii tax, about US$15–20 per night (roughly US$105–140 for the week).
  • Westin Kierland (Scottsdale): no resort fee. The resort may add a small Arizona room tax at checkout, about US$10 per night.
  • Garza Blanca (Puerto Vallarta): Jalisco state lodging tax (3–4%, based on the resort's own room rate) plus a tourism fee of about US$25 per stay.

These amounts are set by the resorts and governments and can change. We'll confirm the current figure for your week before you book.

Is booking direct cheaper than RedWeek?

Usually, yes. Our weeks are also listed on RedWeek, where renters pay RedWeek's service fee on top of the rental price. Booking direct is likely lower because there are no commission fees. RedWeek is still available if you prefer to pay by credit card or want its booking protection.

How does booking work?

Send an inquiry. We send a rental agreement to sign and need your full legal name, address as shown on your ID, email and phone number, because the resort checks photo ID at check-in. A 50% deposit is due when you sign. We then put the reservation in your name with the resort and send you the confirmation (you can call the resort to verify). The balance is due once the reservation is in your name.

How do I pay?

By Zelle, Interac e-Transfer (we provide the Canadian dollar equivalent) or PayPal Friends & Family. All amounts are in US dollars, and any transaction fees are paid by the payer.

What is the cancellation policy?

All bookings are final. There are no cancellations or refunds once the reservation has been transferred into your name.

What are the villas like?

One- and two-bedroom resort villas with full resort amenities. Two-bedroom villas at the Westin resorts sleep up to 8 and have a full kitchen and in-unit washer and dryer. Any fees the resort itself charges at check-in, such as Hawaii state occupancy fees, are separate and paid to the resort.
